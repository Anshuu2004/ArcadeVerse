## 📋 Multiple Contributor Same-Game Rule
If multiple contributors wish to build the same type of game (for instance, **Snake** or **Tetris**):
- You are free to do so!
- Your folder must be distinguished by adding your handle or unique style, e.g.:
  - `games/snake-(your-name)/`
  - `games/tetris-(your-name)/`
- This ensures **both contributors get full credit and zero git conflict**, and players can enjoy and compare both versions!

*Keep building and contributing! 🚀*
